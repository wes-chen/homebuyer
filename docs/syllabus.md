# Syllabus — 14 weekly lessons

## Arc 1 — The money (lessons 1–5)
1. Sizing your budget: the affordability math (28/36 rule, PITI, cash to close)
2. Down payment: how much, where it comes from, PMI
3. Mortgage types: fixed vs ARM, conforming vs jumbo (Bay Area = jumbo territory)
4. The true monthly cost: PITI + HOA + maintenance (the 1% rule)
5. Closing costs, cash to close, pre-approval vs pre-qualification

## Arc 2 — The search (lessons 6–9)
6. South Bay vs Peninsula: neighborhood-level price geography
7. Reading a listing: what photos hide, disclosures, days on market
8. Open houses: what to look at, what to ask, red flags
9. Comps: how to tell if a price is real

## Arc 3 — The process (lessons 10–13)
10. Offers: price, contingencies, escalation clauses, Bay Area bidding dynamics
11. Inspections and disclosures: what actually kills deals
12. Escrow to closing: timeline and who does what
13. After closing: Prop 13, insurance, maintenance reserves

## Capstone (lesson 14)
14. Your personal buy-box: budget + criteria → the targeted listing search goes live
